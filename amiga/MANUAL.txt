                    DANGER FROM THE DEEP (AmigaOS 4.1)
                                USER MANUAL

Welcome to the native AmigaOS 4.1 port of "Danger from the Deep" (dftd)!
This simulation puts you on the bridge of a German submarine during World
War II, featuring realistic physics simulation, detailed 3D graphics, celestial
mechanics, acoustic sensors, and convoy battle tactics.

1. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
- Operating System: AmigaOS 4.1 Final Edition (Update 1 or Update 2 recommended)
- Hardware: AmigaOne X1000, X5000, A1222+, Sam460cr/ex or compatible PowerPC system
- Graphics Card: RadeonHD or RadeonRX with active 3D hardware acceleration
  (Warp3D Nova and Mesa 3D / OpenGL ES)
- Memory: Minimum 1 GB RAM recommended
- Audio: AHI-compatible sound system
- Stack: A 4 MB stack cookie is embedded into the executable ($STACK: 4194304).
  Manual stack adjustment in the shell or icon is normally not required.


================================================================================
2. INSTALLATION
================================================================================
1. Extract the LHA archive to any location on your hard drive
   (e.g., "Work:Games/DangerFromTheDeep" or "DH1:Games/DangerFromTheDeep").
2. Open the drawer on Workbench.
3. Start the game by double-clicking one of the program icons:
   - "DangerFromTheDeep" (recommended for regular gameplay)
   - or "DangerFromTheDeep_Debug" (for troubleshooting with detailed log file).

NOTE ON MESA LIBRARY:
The "libs/" subdirectory contains the required "mesa.library". If your system
does not already have an up-to-date Mesa library installed in "LIBS:", you can
copy this file there if needed.


================================================================================
3. INCLUDED EXECUTABLES
================================================================================
This archive includes two specialized Mesa binaries:

A) "DangerFromTheDeep" (WITHOUT DEBUG - RECOMMENDED FOR REGULAR PLAY)
   ------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Maximum performance: All CPU and compiler optimizations (-O3, AltiVec/VMX
     acceleration for PowerPC) are active.
   - Zero logging overhead: All debug console and file logging is completely
     disabled (no console popups, no file writes).
   - Delivers the highest frame rates (FPS) in the 3D ocean and on the map.

B) "DangerFromTheDeep_Debug" (WITH DEBUG - FOR ANALYSIS & DIAGNOSTICS)
   -------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Comprehensive logging: Writes detailed status and initialization messages
     to "T:dangerdeep_startup.log".
   - Extended diagnostics: In case of unexpected conditions or missing files,
     function call traces and stack information are captured.
   - Ideal for diagnosing graphics driver or sound configuration issues.

4. KEYBOARD CONTROLS & STATIONS
================================================================================

STATIONS & CAMERAS (F1 to F10):
-------------------------------
[F1]   Central Control Room & Gauges (Helm, Depth Gauges, Compass)
[F2]   Periscope (Attack & Observation)
[F3]   UZO (Submarine Target Optics on the Bridge for surface night attacks)
[F4]   Free Bridge View (Surface watch)
[F5]   Navigation & Situation Map (World map, convoys, sensor contacts)
[F6]   Torpedo Room (Tube allocation, reloading, preheating, pistol settings)
[F7]   Damage Control Room (Compartment damage status, bilge pumps)
[F8]   War Diary / Logbook (KTB)
[F9]   Sunk Tonnage Record
[F10]  Free External Camera

SUBMARINE CONTROLS (HELM & ENGINES):
------------------------------------
[Cursor Left / Right]   Rudder Port / Starboard
[Cursor Up / Down]      Dive Planes (Down / Up)
[Shift + Cursor Keys]   Faster rudder movement
[Enter / Return]        Rudder amidships (center rudder)

[1]   Ahead Slow
[2]   Ahead Half
[3]   Ahead Flank (Maximum speed)
[4]   Stop Engines
[5]   Astern Slow
[6]   Astern Full

DIVING COMMANDS & DEPTH CONTROL:
--------------------------------
[s]   Surface (Blow ballast tanks, full surface transit)
[p]   Periscope depth (approx. 12-14 meters depending on submarine type)
[d]   Snorkel depth (if snorkel equipped)
[c]   ALARM! / Crash dive (flood all negative tanks, steep dive angle)
[0]   Raise / lower periscope (0 on main keyboard)
[f]   Raise / lower snorkel

TARGETING & WEAPONS:
--------------------
[Space]             Lock / track target ship
[i]                 Identify target (open Recognition Manual)
[h]                 Turn submarine heading towards current view direction
[v]                 Align view direction to current compass heading
[t]                 Fire torpedo (automatic tube selection)
[Shift + 1..6]      Fire specific torpedo tube 1 to 6
[z]                 Toggle optical zoom level (binoculars / periscope)
[g]                 Manned deck gun view / fire
[Shift + g]         Exit deck gun station

NAVIGATION MAP (F5):
--------------------
[+] / [-]           Zoom in / out on map
[Arrow Keys]        Scroll map view

TIME COMPRESSION & SYSTEM:
--------------------------
[F11]               Increase time compression (1x -> 2x -> 4x -> 8x -> 16x -> 32x)
[F12]               Decrease time compression
[Pause]             Pause / unpause simulation
[Esc]               Pause & quit menu
[Print Screen]      Save screenshot

5. STARTING MISSIONS & SAVE GAMES
================================================================================

MAIN MENU:
- Single Mission:
  * "Convoy Battle": Large convoy with escorts and merchant freighters.
  * "Historical Missions": Predefined historical scenarios and tactical drills.
  * "Saved Game": Resume saved submarine patrols.
- Mission Editor: Create custom naval scenarios.
- Ships & Airplanes: 3D showcase of all historical ships, planes, and weapons.
- Options: Language, graphic details, sound volume.

SAVING & LOADING:
- During gameplay, open the pause menu [Esc] to save your patrol.
- In the save dialogue, type any name. Keyboard input (letters, numbers, space,
  backspace, delete, and arrow keys) is fully supported.
- Saved games are stored in "PROGDIR:save/".

6. TACTICAL TIPS FOR SUBMARINE COMMANDERS
================================================================================
- BEWARE OF DESTROYERS:
  Escort vessels (Tribal class, Javelin, Flower-class corvettes) carry active
  ASDIC (sonar) and depth charges. When detected, dive deep immediately (> 100 m)
  and reduce speed to silent running (speed 1 or 2) to minimize radiated noise.

- NIGHT ATTACKS:
  At night, a surfaced submarine is very difficult to spot visually. Approach
  convoys on the surface at medium speed and use the UZO [F3] for precision
  torpedo shots from 1000-1500 meters.

- TORPEDO MANAGEMENT [F6]:
  Reloading torpedo tubes takes considerable time and is slower in heavy seas
  or at high speeds. Pre-heat electric torpedoes before firing to maximize
  reliability and torpedo speed.

- DAMAGE CONTROL [F7]:
  Flooding must be countered immediately by closing bulkheads and activating
  bilge pumps before the excess water weight pulls the boat past its crush depth.

7. GRAPHICS SETTINGS & PERFORMANCE
================================================================================
- Command line options via Amiga Shell:
  DangerFromTheDeep --res 1024*768       (Standard 4:3 resolution, recommended)
  DangerFromTheDeep --res 1280*720       (Widescreen 720p)
  DangerFromTheDeep --nofullscreen       (Windowed mode on Workbench)
  DangerFromTheDeep --nosound            (Run without sound)
  DangerFromTheDeep --maxfps 60          (Limit frame rate to 60 FPS)

- In the in-game options menu, water reflections, spray particle effects,
  and terrain mesh detail can be adjusted for optimal performance.


================================================================================
8. TROUBLESHOOTING & DIAGNOSTICS
================================================================================
1. GAME DOES NOT START / 3D ERROR:
   Ensure 3D hardware drivers are properly installed for your Radeon card
   ("Warp3D Nova" and "mesa.library"). A compatible "mesa.library" is included
   in the "libs/" directory.

2. CHECKING THE LOG FILE:
   If the game terminates unexpectedly, launch "DangerFromTheDeep_Debug" (the debug
   version). Then inspect "T:dangerdeep_startup.log" to see the exact cause
   (e.g. video memory exhaustion or missing assets).

3. AUDIO ISSUES:
   Verify that AmigaOS audio preferences (AHI) have a valid sound unit
   configured (e.g., Envy24HT on AmigaOne X1000).
